Terms and Conditions

Eligibility

  • Coaching access is open to U.S. residents, whether applying individually or through a company or nonprofit partner's sponsored stipend.
  • Applicants must be either currently employed or actively pursuing professional growth, as defined in the application form.

Application Guidelines

  • All individual applications must be submitted through the official form.
  • Company and nonprofit partnerships are established through a separate partnership agreement.
  • Information provided must be accurate and complete. Misleading or incomplete submissions may result in disqualification.

Data Collection & Privacy

  • Use of Data: Personal information is collected solely for the purpose of evaluating applications and administering coaching access.
  • Privacy Compliance: We follow all applicable U.S. privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).
  • Third-Party Sharing: We do not share applicant data with third parties without explicit consent, unless required by law.

Use of Coaching Access

  • Recipients must use coaching resources solely for professional development as outlined in their application or sponsoring organization's agreement.
  • Proof of participation or use may be requested to ensure alignment and impact.

Accuracy & Accountability

  • By submitting an application, you certify that all information is true and accurate to the best of your knowledge.
  • Providing false or misleading information may result in disqualification or forfeiture of benefits.

Access Limitations

  • Coaching access is subject to available funding and coach capacity.
  • Future Leaders Movement reserves the right to modify, pause, or discontinue any part of coaching access at any time without prior notice.

Participation Requirements

  • Attendance: Participants are expected to attend all scheduled coaching sessions. Two (2) unexcused absences may result in removal from coaching access. Future Leaders Movement may also charge up to $300 per missed session to recover the cost of coaching time reserved on your behalf.
  • Punctuality: Repeated lateness (for example, arriving more than 10 minutes late multiple times) may indicate lack of engagement and may result in disqualification.
  • Engagement: Failure to respond to communication, complete agreed-upon tasks, or demonstrate active participation may lead to removal at the discretion of Future Leaders Movement.
  • Agreement: All selected participants must sign a participation agreement confirming these terms before beginning coaching.

Acknowledgment

By submitting your application, you:

  • Confirm that you have read and agree to these Terms and Conditions
  • Commit to participating fully if selected
  • Consent to the collection and use of your information as outlined above
