About Coaching Access

We meet you where you are. Through pro bono or highly discounted 1:1 and group coaching, targeted career tools, and strategic guidance, we help you move forward, on your terms.

Two Ways In

We offer coaching access in two ways:

  1. Individual Applications — Individuals can apply directly for pro bono or discounted coaching, based on available funding.
  2. Company & Nonprofit Sponsorship — Organizations can sponsor coaching stipends for their employees or members, extending access to more people at once. (See For Companies & Nonprofits)

Personalized Coaching and Mentorship

You'll be paired with a seasoned coach carefully matched to your goals, career stage, and lived experience. Your coach becomes a trusted thought partner, offering clarity, strategy, and honest guidance every step of the way.

Job Search & Launch Support

From resume and LinkedIn refinement to interview prep and decision-making tools, we help you tell your story, stand out in a crowded market, and land what's next.

Group Coaching

Alongside 1:1 support, we offer group coaching for participants who benefit from shared learning and peer accountability, often at a lower cost of access.

Career Direction & Growth Planning

Your coach helps you map your next move, whatever that looks like: a pivot, a step into leadership, or simply a clearer path forward.

Who Should Apply

Coaching access is designed for ambitious individuals who have faced barriers to leadership and are:

  • Seeking to advance their careers and increase their earning potential
  • Looking to make a transition into a new professional field
  • Aiming to overcome barriers and create new opportunities
  • Committed to personal and professional growth
  • Passionate about being a catalyst for positive change within their communities
  • Dedicated to giving back and lifting others as they climb
