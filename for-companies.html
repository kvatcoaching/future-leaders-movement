Invest in the Leaders Already on Your Team

Your emerging leaders and underrepresented employees have the ambition. What they often lack is access: to coaching, to mentorship, to the kind of guidance that turns potential into promotion.

Future Leaders Movement partners with companies, nonprofits, and foundations to expand coaching access, whether that means sponsoring stipends for your own people or supporting our work more broadly.

How It Works

  1. You sponsor a stipend. Your organization funds coaching access for a set number of employees or members.
  2. We match them with a coach. Each participant is paired with a coach based on their background, goals, and career stage.
  3. Your team grows. Participants build the skills and confidence to lead, and your bench gets stronger as a result.

Why Partner With Us

  • Strengthen retention and internal mobility by investing visibly in underrepresented talent
  • Support DEI and leadership development goals with hands-on, measurable coaching access
  • Offer a benefit your employees will actually use and remember
  • Partner with a nonprofit with a track record: over 200 hours of coaching delivered and growing

Ways to Partner

  • Sponsor coaching stipends for employees or members
  • Offer hiring pathways or job shadowing opportunities for participants
  • Collaborate on events, workshops, or thought leadership
  • Support the tools and resources that help participants take their next step
  • Fund coaching access more broadly as a foundation or community partner, without sponsoring a specific team

Whether you're a company sponsoring coaching for your team, a nonprofit extending support to your members, or a foundation funding access more broadly, we'll build a structure around your goals and budget.
