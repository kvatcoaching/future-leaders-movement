Apply for Coaching Access

Apply to receive pro bono or discounted 1:1 coaching support, matched to your goals and background.

Note: if your employer or a nonprofit you're part of already partners with us, ask them about their sponsored stipend, since that may get you access faster or at a further discount.

Who Is Eligible to Apply

Open to individuals from all backgrounds who meet the following criteria:

  • Identify as someone who has faced systemic barriers to opportunity. Examples include (but are not limited to) women, people of color, LGBTQIA+ individuals, first-generation professionals, people with disabilities, or others who face systemic barriers.
  • Are 18 years or older and proficient in English
  • Demonstrate a clear commitment to personal and professional growth
  • Reside in the United States
  • Please carefully read all Terms & Conditions before applying

FAQ

What is coaching access?
It's access to pro bono (fully free) or discounted 1:1 or group coaching and mentorship, aimed at supporting underrepresented individuals in developing leadership skills. We match you with a coach based on your goals and background.
What happens after my coaching engagement ends?
You'll have the option to continue working with your coach at a discounted rate. There's no obligation to continue. If you choose not to, we're happy to refer you to other low-cost resources, or you're welcome to reapply for future coaching opportunities.
How flexible is coaching access?
Very flexible. Coaching is self-paced and scheduled around your existing commitments. It's not full-time.
What happens if I'm not selected for pro bono coaching?
You may still be eligible for discounted coaching through our Partial-Funding track.
Is my application information confidential?
Yes. All submitted information is kept private and only reviewed by our selection committee.
Can my employer sponsor my coaching?
Yes. If your company or a nonprofit you belong to partners with us, they can sponsor a stipend that covers or discounts your coaching. Learn more about company partnerships.
