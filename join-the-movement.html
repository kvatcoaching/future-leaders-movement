Two Ways In

We offer coaching access in two ways:

  1. Individual Applications — Individuals can apply directly for pro bono or discounted coaching, based on available funding.
  2. Company & Nonprofit Sponsorship — Organizations can sponsor coaching stipends for their employees or members, extending access to more people at once. (See For Companies & Nonprofits)

Note: if your employer or a nonprofit you're part of already partners with us, ask them about their sponsored stipend, since that may get you access faster or at a further discount.

What Coaching Access Includes

Personalized Coaching and Mentorship

You'll be paired with a seasoned coach carefully matched to your goals, career stage, and lived experience. Your coach becomes a trusted thought partner, offering clarity, strategy, and honest guidance every step of the way.

Job Search & Launch Support

From resume and LinkedIn refinement to interview prep and decision-making tools, we help you tell your story, stand out in a crowded market, and land what's next.

Group Coaching

Alongside 1:1 support, we offer group coaching for participants who benefit from shared learning and peer accountability, often at a lower cost of access.

Career Direction & Growth Planning

Your coach helps you map your next move, whatever that looks like: a pivot, a step into leadership, or simply a clearer path forward.

Who Should Apply

Coaching access is designed for ambitious individuals who have faced barriers to leadership and are:

  • Seeking to advance their careers and increase their earning potential
  • Looking to make a transition into a new professional field
  • Aiming to overcome barriers and create new opportunities
  • Committed to personal and professional growth
  • Passionate about being a catalyst for positive change within their communities
  • Dedicated to giving back and lifting others as they climb

Who Is Eligible to Apply

Open to individuals from all backgrounds who meet the following criteria:

  • Identify as someone who has faced systemic barriers to opportunity. Examples include (but are not limited to) women, people of color, LGBTQIA+ individuals, first-generation professionals, people with disabilities, or others who face systemic barriers.
  • Are 18 years or older and proficient in English
  • Demonstrate a clear commitment to personal and professional growth
  • Reside in the United States
  • Please carefully read all Terms & Conditions before applying

FAQ

What is coaching access?
It's access to pro bono (fully free) or discounted 1:1 or group coaching and mentorship, aimed at supporting traditionally overlooked individuals in developing leadership skills. We match you with a coach based on your goals and background.
What happens after my coaching engagement ends?
You'll have the option to continue working with your coach at a discounted rate. There's no obligation to continue. If you choose not to, we're happy to refer you to other low-cost resources, or you're welcome to reapply for future coaching opportunities.
How flexible is coaching access?
Very flexible. Coaching is self-paced and scheduled around your existing commitments. It's not full-time.
What happens if I'm not selected for pro bono coaching?
You may still be eligible for discounted coaching through our Partial-Funding track.
Is my application information confidential?
Yes. All submitted information is kept private and only reviewed by our selection committee.
Can my employer sponsor my coaching?
Yes. If your company or a nonprofit you belong to partners with us, they can sponsor a stipend that covers or discounts your coaching. Learn more about company partnerships.
